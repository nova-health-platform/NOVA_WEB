Tips to reduce burnout

  • Take regular breaks and disconnect outside work hours
  • Set realistic goals and manage expectations
  • Practice mindfulness, breathing exercises, or light activity
  • Seek social support from colleagues, friends or family
  • Speak with a supervisor about workload or role clarity
  • Consider speaking with a coach, therapist, or counselor
