Understanding the confidence score

What is Confidence?

The confidence score represents how certain our AI system is about the diagnostic prediction. It's calculated based on the strength of the pattern matching between your symptoms and the medical knowledge database.

Low (0-30%)

Uncertain prediction, multiple possibilities

Moderate (30-70%)

Likely prediction with some uncertainty

High (70-100%)

Strong prediction with high certainty

Important Note

This confidence score is based on AI analysis and should not replace professional medical diagnosis. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider for proper medical evaluation.
