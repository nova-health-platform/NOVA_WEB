Getting Started

Account Creation

To start using Nova, you need to create an account. Click on "Register" in the header and fill in your information:

  • • First and Last Name
  • • Email Address
  • • Password (with confirmation)
  • • Phone Number (optional)
  • • Country
  • • Consent to Terms of Use and HIPAA compliance

First Login

After registration, you can log in using your email and password. Nova will guide you through the initial setup process.

User Workflow

1

Symptom Input

Start by describing your symptoms in the analysis interface. You can either:

  • • Type your symptoms directly in the text input
  • • Select from suggested symptoms
  • • Use voice input (if available)
2

Patient Information

Provide your demographic information for more accurate analysis:

  • • Age, Gender, Weight, Height
  • • Select from existing profiles (if logged in)
  • • Create new profile for future use
3

Clarification Questions

Nova may ask follow-up questions to better understand your condition:

  • • Answer Yes/No questions about symptoms
  • • Provide additional details when prompted
  • • Indicate pain locations on body diagrams
4

Analysis Results

Receive comprehensive analysis including:

  • • Quick Summary with confidence level
  • • Detailed disease information
  • • Treatment recommendations
  • • Demographic insights and risk factors

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Nova a replacement for medical professionals?

No, Nova is not a replacement for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It provides preliminary analysis and recommendations to help you make informed decisions about your health. Always consult with qualified healthcare providers for medical concerns.

How accurate are Nova's analyses?

Nova uses advanced AI algorithms trained on medical data to provide preliminary assessments. Each analysis includes a confidence level to help you understand the reliability of the results. However, accuracy can vary based on the information provided and the complexity of the condition.

Can I use Nova for emergency situations?

No, Nova should never be used for emergency medical situations. If you're experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services immediately or go to the nearest emergency room.

How is my health data protected?

Nova implements industry-standard security measures and complies with HIPAA regulations where applicable. Your health data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your personal health information with third parties without your explicit consent.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Your access to premium features will continue until the end of your current billing period.
