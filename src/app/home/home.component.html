FAQ

Any Questions?

Do you have any questions? We have the answers!

Does Nova replace medical advice?

No. Nova provides information and recommendations for guidance purposes only through AI. For diagnosis, treatment, or emergencies, consult a healthcare professional.

How does symptom analysis work?

You describe your symptoms, and Nova cross-references this information with your profile (age, history, allergies, etc.) and machine-learning models to suggest interpretations and useful resources.

Is the personalized medical profile mandatory?

No, but it significantly improves the relevance of the results (allergies, treatments, medical history, etc.). You can gradually add to it and modify it at any time.

Is my data protected?

Yes. We apply strict security and confidentiality standards. Your data is never resold, and you can export or delete it from your account.

What tools does Nova offer?

A personalized medical profile, symptom analysis, psychological monitoring, a dermatological check-up, and recommendations tailored to your country.

Is Nova available in my country?

Yes, Nova adapts its recommendations by country (local resources, useful information). Some features may vary depending on local regulations.

Is there a free version? Subscriptions?

Nova offers a free plan and paid plans with advanced features (extended history, additional analytics, prioritization, etc.).

Can I share my results with my doctor?

Yes. You can export or copy your results to share them with a healthcare professional.
