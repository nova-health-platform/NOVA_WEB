DASS-21 – Mental Health Assessment

The DASS-21 is a set of three self-report scales designed to measure the emotional states of depression, anxiety and stress. It is a reliable and valid tool used worldwide.

Why take the DASS-21?

  • Evaluate your emotional well-being
  • Track symptoms of depression, anxiety, and stress
  • Evidence-based self-assessment tool
  • Confidential and accessible anytime
⚠️ This is not a diagnostic tool. It’s a self-assessment to help you understand your emotional state. For medical advice, please consult a professional.

Understanding your results

  • Depression: Normal (0–9), Mild (10–13), Moderate (14–20), Severe (21–27), Extremely Severe (28+)
  • Anxiety: Normal (0–7), Mild (8–9), Moderate (10–14), Severe (15–19), Extremely Severe (20+)
  • Stress: Normal (0–14), Mild (15–18), Moderate (19–25), Severe (26–33), Extremely Severe (34+)
