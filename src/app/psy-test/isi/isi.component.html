ISI – Insomnia Severity Index

The ISI is a brief self-report questionnaire used to assess the severity of insomnia symptoms in adults. It is widely used in clinical and research settings.

Why take the ISI?

  • Assess your sleep quality and disturbances
  • Identify potential insomnia symptoms
  • Receive personalized feedback based on your responses
  • Confidential, quick, and evidence-based
⚠️ This tool is not a medical diagnosis. It's a self-assessment designed to help you reflect on your sleep health. For any persistent issues, consult a healthcare provider.

Understanding your results

  • 0 – 7 : No clinically significant insomnia
  • 8 – 14 : Subthreshold insomnia
  • 15 – 21 : Clinical insomnia (moderate)
  • 22 – 28 : Clinical insomnia (severe)
