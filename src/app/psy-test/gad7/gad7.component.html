About GAD-7

The GAD-7 is a clinically validated questionnaire used to assess the severity of generalized anxiety disorder. It is widely used in both clinical and research settings.

Why take the GAD-7?

  • Understand your anxiety level
  • Identify if you may benefit from support
  • Use it to start a conversation with a professional
  • Fast, private, and based on clinical evidence
⚠️

Important Notice

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. If you're struggling, seek help from a qualified mental health professional.

Understanding your results

0–4 (Minimal): Little to no anxiety symptoms. No specific action required, but healthy habits are encouraged.

5–9 (Mild): Mild anxiety symptoms. Watchful waiting, stress reduction, and lifestyle changes may be helpful.

10–14 (Moderate): Anxiety symptoms may warrant further evaluation. Consider speaking with a professional.

15–21 (Severe): High levels of anxiety. Professional mental health intervention is likely needed.
