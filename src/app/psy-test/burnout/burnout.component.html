Burnout Self-Assessment

This burnout questionnaire helps identify signs of emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, and reduced personal accomplishment. It is inspired by tools such as the Maslach Burnout Inventory.

Why take this assessment?

  • Gauge your risk of burnout
  • Take early action to prevent worsening
  • Reflect on your work-related stress
  • Fast, anonymous, and evidence-informed
⚠️ This tool is not a diagnosis. It provides guidance based on your answers. If you're overwhelmed, talk to a healthcare provider.

Understanding your results

  • 0 – 18 : Low risk of burnout — maintain self-care and balance.
  • 19 – 36 : Moderate signs of burnout — reflect, set limits, and consider adjustments.
  • 37 – 54 : High risk — reduce load, prioritize rest, seek support or counseling.

Tips to reduce burnout

  • Take regular breaks and disconnect outside work hours
  • Set realistic goals and manage expectations
  • Practice mindfulness, breathing exercises, or light activity
  • Seek social support from colleagues, friends or family
  • Speak with a supervisor about workload or role clarity
  • Consider speaking with a coach, therapist, or counselor

This is not a diagnosis. If you're feeling overwhelmed, talk to a health or mental health professional.
